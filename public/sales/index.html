Questions

Straight answers

What do I need to get a demo site?

Your Arizona ROC license number and a phone number or email. We pull your business name, classification and city from the public ROC record and build the demo from that. If you're handyman-exempt, give us your business name and city instead.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Every site is plain HTML on your own domain. If you leave, you take the files and the domain with you. There's no WordPress install, no plugins and no proprietary builder holding your content.

Where do my leads go?

The quote form emails you within seconds and keeps every lead in your own portal, where you can mark it contacted, add notes and download a spreadsheet any time. The Full Marketing Kit adds SMS alerts to your phone.

I'm a handyman without a ROC license. Can I still get a site?

Yes. Sites for unlicensed work include Arizona's handyman exemption disclosure so you stay on the right side of the statute, and the ROC badge is replaced with your actual credentials.

Can I edit the site myself?

Pro and Kit sites include a browser editor: click any text on your own site, change it, and publish. Photos are managed from a Google Drive folder — drop images in and the gallery rebuilds itself.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, SSL, the lead-capture backend, uptime monitoring, and content changes. Cancel any time; the site stays yours.

Will this rank me #1 on Google?

Nobody can promise that, and you should walk away from anyone who does. What we do: give Google and AI assistants clean, structured information about your business, a page for every service and city, and a site that loads fast on phones. That's the foundation the map pack and organic results are built on.

How is this different from Wix or GoDaddy?

Those give you a blank builder. We give you a finished contractor site with the Arizona-specific pieces — ROC verify link, license class, exemption disclosure, service-area pages — already done, and a lead form that's tested to actually deliver. You also don't pay per plugin.
