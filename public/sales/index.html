Arizona advertising rules

Your website can carry your license number for your ads.

Since September 2021, Arizona lets certain ads leave off the ROC number when they show your web address — as long as that site's home page displays your business name and license number prominently, and not just in the footer.

Every site we build puts both in the header and on the first screen of the home page, with a one-tap link to your record at azroc.gov. That's what makes a short yard sign or a clean truck door work.

General information from the Registrar's notice, not legal advice. Bids, contracts, estimates and correspondence still need your license number. Read the ROC notice ↗

Questions

Straight answers

How fast is the free demo?

Usually under a minute after you tap Build. Your link appears right on this page, and we email it to you too. If your license isn't on the ROC active list yet, we build it by hand within one business day.

What do I need to get a demo site?

Your Arizona ROC license number and an email address. We read your business name, license class and city from the Registrar's active-license list. Handyman-exempt? Give us your business name and city instead.

What happens after I pay?

Your demo turns into your paid site within minutes. A three-minute setup form lets you pick your services, the cities you work in, your hours and your domain, and the site rebuilds itself. No phone tag.

Is there a contract?

No. You pay the setup fee once, then a flat monthly that starts after your site goes live. Cancel any time from the billing portal and take your files and domain with you.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Every site is plain HTML on your own domain. If you leave, you take the files and the domain with you. There's no WordPress install, no plugins and no proprietary builder holding your content.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Type it into the setup form. We connect it on our side and email you the two DNS records to add where you bought it. HTTPS turns on by itself once those records take effect.

Where do my leads go?

Quote requests land in your inbox within seconds and are kept in your own portal, where you can mark them contacted and download a spreadsheet any time. The Full Marketing Kit adds text alerts to your phone.

I'm a handyman without a ROC license. Can I still get a site?

Yes. Sites for unlicensed work include Arizona's handyman exemption disclosure, and the ROC badge is replaced with your actual credentials.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, HTTPS, the lead-capture backend, uptime monitoring and content changes. Cancel any time; the site stays yours.

Will this rank me #1 on Google?

Nobody can promise that, and you should walk away from anyone who does. We give Google and AI assistants clean, structured information about your business, a page for every service and city, and a site that loads fast on phones. That is the foundation the map pack and organic results are built on.
